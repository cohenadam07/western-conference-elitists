## Findings

- **Roles.** The live feed's 17 participant `type` values map one-to-one onto the release file's 17 `*_player_id(s)` column families (camelCase to snake_case). Nothing on either side is unmapped. Every participant carried an athlete id (0 without).
- **Play ids.** Same ids on both sides. Every release row matches a live play. Every live play that has a participant is in the release participants file, except one in 401856736 (play 401856736318, a rush wiped out by offsetting penalties, `NO PLAY`, absent from both release files). The live feed has 5-6 more plays per game than the release pbp.
- **Betting lines.** `pickcenter` (DraftKings) is present for the five 2026 games and empty for the five 2025 games; `odds` is empty for all ten. Drives, win probability and the box score are present for all ten.
- **Athlete ids.** 3493 of 3505 play-role cells identical (99.7%); 2025 games 100%. All 12 disagreements are in two 2026 games: in 10 the release has a tackler/sacker the live feed no longer returns (the text names that player), and in 2 the live feed has since corrected a wrong-jersey id the release still holds. Cause not established (the release was scraped at some earlier moment; ESPN edits plays afterwards).
- **Interceptions.** The intercepting defender is `passDefender` and, when there is a return, also `returner` (same id). 14 of 16 interception plays had it; the 2 without were touchback interceptions with only a `passer`. `passDefender` therefore mixes break-ups and interceptions; the play type separates them.
- **QB hurries.** No role in either source. Text only (stat-crew format), or the box score's per-player `hurries`.
- **Tackles for loss.** No role in either source. Derive from `tackler`/`assistedBy` on plays with negative yardage, or take `tacklesForLoss` from the box score.
- **Fumbles.** `forcedBy`, `fumbler`, `recoverer` appeared in the 2026 games and in none of the five 2025 games, which were all in ESPN's short text format; see table (e) for the season-wide picture.
- **Caveat on the sample.** The first five event ids on each date are all SEC games; section 5 repeats the comparison on two games per date for eight other conferences: 2025: 14 games, 100.0% of 4786 play-role cells identical, tackler/assist on 92.0% of rushes and completions; 2026: 14 games, 99.4% of 4919 play-role cells identical, tackler/assist on 90.7% of rushes and completions.
- **Games missing from the release files (section 6).** ESPN has plays for all eight on the core plays endpoint (`count` 67-201), but the summary has no `drives` and no win probability for 8 of them; 3 have fewer plays than any of the 38 complete games sampled above (minimum 146), so they may be partial: 401282608, 401281943, 401282612.
