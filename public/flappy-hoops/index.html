Add to Home Screen

iPhone doesn't let websites go full screen. Put Flappy Hoops on your Home Screen instead and it opens like its own app, with no browser bars.

  1. In Safari, tap ··· next to the address bar, then Share. (On older iPhones, tap Share at the bottom.)
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. Keep Open as Web App on, then tap Add.
  3. Play from the Flappy Hoops icon. The app keeps its own save, separate from Safari’s, so it starts you at New York.
